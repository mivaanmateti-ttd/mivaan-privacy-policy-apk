Mivaan – TTD Auto Fill

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 31 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Mivaan – TTD Auto Fill (“Mivaan”, “we”, “our” or “the app”) handles information when you use the app.

1. Information you enter

Mivaan is designed to let you save pilgrim and booking-profile details on your device so you can access them when preparing supported TTD booking forms. Depending on the fields you choose to save, this information may include names, contact details and other booking-related details you enter.

2. Where information is stored

Saved pilgrim and profile information is stored locally on your device. Mivaan does not operate its own cloud account or server for storing these saved profiles. We do not receive a copy of the locally saved profile information through the app.

Your device’s operating system, backup settings, or other apps you use may separately back up or access device data according to their own settings and policies.

3. How information is used

Information you save is used to display your profiles and help you enter details into supported booking forms at your direction. The app does not itself guarantee ticket availability, a successful booking, or payment completion.

4. Sharing and disclosure

Mivaan does not sell your saved profile information. Mivaan does not transmit locally saved profile information to us. If you choose to use another website or service and enter or submit information there, that information is handled by that third party under its own privacy policy and terms.

5. Permissions and access

The app may request device permissions only where needed for its features. You can review and manage app permissions in your device settings. Do not save information that you do not want stored on your device.

6. Data retention and deletion

Your saved profiles remain on your device until you delete them within the app, clear the app’s data, or uninstall the app. Uninstalling the app may remove locally stored information, subject to your device and backup settings.

7. Children’s privacy

Mivaan is a general-purpose utility and is not designed specifically for children. Parents or guardians should supervise children’s use of the app and the information they enter.

8. Security

We take a local-storage approach to avoid maintaining a central store of saved pilgrim profiles. However, no method of storage or electronic transmission is completely secure. Keep your device protected with an appropriate screen lock and do not share it with people you do not trust.

9. Third-party services and official TTD website

Mivaan is an independent utility and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or officially connected to Tirumala Tirupati Devasthanams (TTD) or the Government of Andhra Pradesh. When you visit the official TTD website or another third-party service, its own privacy policy applies.

Official TTD website: https://ttdevasthanams.ap.gov.in/

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app’s features or data practices change. The revised policy will be published at this same URL with an updated effective date.
